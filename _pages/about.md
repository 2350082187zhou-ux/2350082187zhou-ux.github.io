**Welcome to my website!**

## About Me

I received my Bachelor's degree in **Electrical Engineering and Automation** from **Anhui Polytechnic University**, Wuhu, China, in 2025. I am currently pursuing a **Ph.D. in Computer Science** at the **University of Nevada, Reno (UNR)**, USA, under the supervision of [Dr. Niusen Chen](https://www.unr.edu/cse/people/niusen-chen).

My research interests include **computer systems security, operating systems, storage systems, AI agent security, and hardware security**. My current research investigates the security risks posed by AI agents with elevated privileges, evaluates host-side protection mechanisms at the file-system, kernel, and block-device layers, and explores stronger security boundaries for protecting sensitive data against unauthorized modification and deletion.

## Research Interests

- **AI Agent Security:** Security risks posed by AI agents with elevated permissions, including unauthorized file modification or deletion.
- **Operating Systems and Kernel Security:** Linux security mechanisms, access control, and kernel-level enforcement.
- **Storage and Hardware Security:** Data integrity and protection mechanisms beyond the host software stack.
- **Security Analysis with AI:** Applications of machine learning and program analysis to software security.

## Selected Research Project

### When AI Agents Go Rogue: Who Protects Your Files?

In this project, I investigate whether host-controlled protection mechanisms can safeguard sensitive files when an AI agent has root privileges. I examine defenses at different levels of the software stack, including **AppArmor**, **eBPF LSM**, and **block-layer enforcement**, to identify the limits of host-side protection and motivate stronger data-integrity boundaries.
